ABOUT THE MONTH

A Month Filled With Mercy & Light

Rabi-ul-Awwal is a month remembered with deep love and reverence by Muslims around the world. It is especially associated with the life and Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The month invites us to reflect upon his character, compassion, patience and teachings, and to bring those beautiful values into our own lives.
